# PAGE - HOME

## Purpose

Create desire, communicate the wellness-resort positioning, and guide the visitor toward exploration and personal enquiry. The homepage should not attempt to explain every detail.

## Authoritative sequence

1. Hero
2. Wellness
3. Arunachala / Location
4. Food / Nourishment
5. Resort
6. Experience
7. Stay
8. Trust
9. Blog
10. Final enquiry CTA
11. Footer

## 01 Hero

Headline direction: `Heal. Reconnect. Renew.`

Supporting direction: a peaceful wellness resort where traditional wellness, healthy food and nature come together in the presence of Arunachala.

CTAs:
- Primary contextual: `Explore Wellness`
- Secondary contextual: `Plan Your Stay`

Use one strong atmospheric image. Arunachala may be present subtly but must not be falsely represented as a confirmed direct property view unless verified.

## 02 Wellness

Headline: `Wellness, in its own time.`

Cover:
- Ayurveda
- traditional therapies
- yoga
- meditation
- healthy food
- nature
- lifestyle

CTA: `Explore Wellness`

The section should feel editorial, not like a clinic menu.

## 03 Arunachala / Location

Headline direction: `In the presence of Arunachala.`

Explain the setting and Tiruvannamalai context in simple language.

Use only client-confirmed geographical statements.

## 04 Food / Nourishment

Headline: `Food that nourishes.`

Communicate farm-grown produce, herbal garden, healthy food and Ayurvedic food principles only where supported.

CTA: `Discover Dining`

No unsupported health claims.

## 05 Resort

Headline direction: `Stay close to nature. Stay close to yourself.`

Show architecture, rooms, gardens and common spaces using real photography when available.

CTA: `Explore the Resort`

## 06 Experience

Headline: `More than a stay.`

Introduce the property's distinctive experience themes without inventing operational details.

CTA: `Explore the Experience`

## 07 Stay

Show only confirmed accommodation categories.

Use temporary development copy/images when needed, tracked in `REPLACEMENTS.md`.

Do not show invented prices or availability.

## 08 Trust

Use only real:
- founder information
- practitioner information
- guest stories
- reviews/ratings
- verified operational facts

Until supplied, use a neutral trust-building layout rather than fake proof.

## 09 Blog

Show up to three featured articles when real articles exist. Otherwise use clearly tracked temporary article placeholders.

## 10 Final CTA

Headline direction: `Your time at Gold Mountain begins here.`

Primary: `WhatsApp Us`
Secondary: `Send an Enquiry`

Show email clearly.

## Footer

Include the approved logo, primary navigation, contact methods, verified address, map link, social links when verified, and legal links when supplied.
