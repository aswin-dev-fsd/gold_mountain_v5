# PAGE - ENQUIRE

## Purpose

Create a premium personal enquiry experience rather than a conventional hotel booking engine.

## Hero

Headline: `Your stay begins with a conversation.`

Supporting direction: tell the team when you would like to visit, what you are looking for and how the stay could be meaningful.

## Form

Fields:
- Name *
- Email *
- WhatsApp / Phone
- Preferred arrival date
- Preferred departure date
- Number of guests
- Interested in: Stay / Wellness / Ayurveda / Monthly Stay / Other
- Message

CTA: `Send Enquiry`

## Direct contact

- WhatsApp: `Chat with us`
- Email: `Send us an email`
- Phone: `Call us` only when a verified number exists

## Hard restrictions

Do not build:
- fake availability
- fake room inventory
- fake package availability
- payment gateway
- automatic booking confirmation
- fake calendar availability

The enquiry is submitted first. The Gold Mountain team confirms availability and pricing directly.
