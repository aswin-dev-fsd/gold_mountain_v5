# CONTENT RULES

## 1. Source of truth

Content must come from the supplied Gold Mountain source material or confirmed client data.

The supplied Website Architecture establishes strategy, positioning, hierarchy and intended themes. It does not provide missing commercial or factual details.

## 2. Never fabricate

Never invent:
- facts about Gold Mountain
- historical claims
- founder biography
- practitioner credentials
- treatment details
- medical outcomes
- room categories
- room capacities
- amenities
- pricing
- package inclusions
- package duration beyond confirmed material
- availability
- testimonials
- review ratings
- awards
- certifications
- distances or travel times
- exact facilities
- contact numbers
- email addresses
- social links

## 3. Temporary copy

For visual-development completeness, Antigravity may write provisional copy when a section cannot be designed without it.

Requirements:
- keep provisional copy generic and non-factual
- never invent credentials, statistics, prices or claims
- record the item in `REPLACEMENTS.md`
- replace it once client copy is available

Temporary copy should be treated as layout copy, not approved marketing copy.

## 4. Tone

Tone:
- calm
- warm
- refined
- simple
- human
- confident without hype
- international

Avoid:
- exaggerated wellness promises
- medical language without evidence
- spiritual pressure
- tourism clichés
- aggressive sales language
- excessive adjectives
- vague luxury clichés

## 5. Ayurveda

Explain Ayurveda in straightforward international English.

Present Ayurveda as one important part of the wider wellness experience.

Do not position Gold Mountain as an Ayurveda clinic.

Do not make unsupported medical claims.

## 6. Arunachala and spirituality

Arunachala is a defining part of the setting and story.

Use respectful, accessible language. Avoid presenting the website as an organization for religious instruction or making unsupported spiritual claims.

The phrase `divine Arunachala` is a strategic source phrase, but visible copy should use it only when it naturally fits the context. Prefer balanced language for international audiences.

## 7. Experience content

Current strategic experience themes include:
- Shiva Shakthi Darshanam
- Cow Shelter
- Fish Pond
- Five Element Philosophy
- Farm-grown vegetables
- Herbal Garden
- Nature walks / Arunachala presence
- Yoga
- Meditation
- Ayurveda
- peaceful surroundings / daily wellness rituals

Only describe these as factual operational offerings when actual client-approved descriptions are available.

## 8. Dining

Food should be presented as nourishment and part of the wellness lifestyle.

Do not claim that a specific food cures, detoxes, reverses, prevents or medically treats a condition without appropriate evidence and client approval.

## 9. Testimonials and trust

Only use real testimonials/reviews supplied or otherwise verified.

Never create fake names, quotes, star ratings, review counts or guest statistics.

## 10. Blog

The Blog replaces Gallery as the strategic content channel.

Categories may include:
- Wellness
- Ayurveda
- Food
- Nature
- Arunachala
- Life at Gold Mountain

Sample article placeholders are allowed for visual development but must be tracked and clearly treated as temporary.

## 11. Pricing

INR is the base/booking currency in the supplied architecture.

USD and EUR are indicative conversions only where confirmed pricing exists.

No invented numbers.

## 12. Forms

Keep forms short and understandable.

Suggested common fields:
- Name
- Email
- WhatsApp / Phone
- Preferred dates
- Number of guests
- Enquiry type
- Message

Never claim a booking is confirmed after form submission.

## 13. Navigation naming

Use `Enquire`, not `Book Now`.

Use `Blog`, not `Gallery`.
