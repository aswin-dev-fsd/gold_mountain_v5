# ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

## Strategy

- Gold Mountain reads as a wellness resort first.
- Ayurveda is important but not dominant.
- The overall story is calm, nature-led and experience-led.
- The homepage follows the approved sequence.
- Navigation is simple and consistent.

## Visual

- Brand palette is recognisable.
- Warm Ivory provides breathing room.
- Forest Green grounds the experience.
- Gold is restrained.
- Deep Red is rare.
- Typography follows Outfit + selective The Seasons use.
- Photography feels natural and premium.
- No glassmorphism or generic SaaS patterns.
- No excessive rounded cards.
- No excessive animation.

## UX

- International visitors can understand what Gold Mountain is.
- Ayurveda is explained simply.
- Location is understandable.
- Stay options are understandable.
- Enquiry route is obvious.
- Email remains available in addition to WhatsApp.
- No fake booking/availability flow exists.

## Content accuracy

- No unverified factual claims are presented as facts.
- Temporary content is tracked.
- Temporary external images are tracked.
- No fake testimonials, reviews, credentials or prices.

## Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation works.
- Focus states are visible.
- Heading hierarchy is logical.
- Forms have labels and useful validation.
- Images have appropriate alt text.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Text contrast is sufficient.

## Responsive

Verify at minimum:
- mobile width
- tablet width
- desktop width
- wide desktop where relevant

Confirm that no important content is clipped or unreadable.

## Technical

Run as applicable:
- TypeScript check
- ESLint
- production build
- route/link verification
- metadata inspection
- image source verification
- accessibility review
- Lighthouse review
- Core Web Vitals review

## Performance

- No unnecessarily large images are shipped.
- Above-the-fold image loading is intentional.
- Below-the-fold images are appropriately lazy-loaded.
- No avoidable client-side JavaScript is introduced.
- Fonts are loaded in a performance-conscious way.
- Third-party scripts are limited.
