# ARCHITECTURE

## Strategic goal

Create an international-standard site that helps guests understand, trust and plan a stay at Gold Mountain Wellness Resort in Tiruvannamalai.

The supplied architecture states that wellness leads, nature surrounds, food nourishes, stay supports and experience defines the journey.

## Positioning

`WELLNESS RESORT`

Not an Ayurveda clinic, not only an Ayurveda retreat, and not simply a hotel.

## Content weighting

- Wellness: 50%
- Location & Nature: 20%
- Food & Nourishment: 15%
- Resort: 10%
- Brand Story: 5%

These are content priorities, not literal screen-space percentages.

## Information architecture

Primary navigation:

- Home
- Wellness
- Stay
- Experience
- About
- Blog
- Contact

Primary header CTA: `Enquire`

Supporting direct contact: WhatsApp

## Homepage architecture - final normalized version

1. Hero
2. Wellness
3. Arunachala / Location
4. Food / Nourishment
5. The Resort

9. Blog / Journal

11. Footer

### 01 Hero
Purpose: first impression; emotional and atmospheric.

Direction:
- Discover Gold Mountain
- atmospheric relationship to Arunachala
- headline direction: `Heal. Reconnect. Renew.`
- primary contextual CTA: `Explore Wellness`
- secondary contextual CTA: `Plan Your Stay`

Do not overcrowd the hero.

### 02 Wellness
The strongest discovery area.

Cover:
- Ayurveda
- Yoga
- Meditation
- Traditional therapies
- Lifestyle
- Healthy food
- Nature
- healing as a way of living

### 03 Arunachala / Location
Explain why the setting is meaningful.

Current architecture states:
- Arunachala to the East
- Parvati Malai to the West
- farm/natural surroundings
- Tiruvannamalai context

Use only claims the client confirms.

### 04 Food / Nourishment
Cover:
- farm-grown vegetables
- herbal garden
- healthy food
- Ayurvedic principles
- food as part of the wellness experience

No unsupported medical claims.

### 05 Resort
Cover:
- accommodation
- nature and surroundings
- facilities/comfort only when confirmed
- hospitality/service

### 06 Experience
Potential areas:
- Shiva Shakthi Darshan
- Five Element Experience/Philosophy
- Cow Shelter
- Fish Pond
- Farm Experience
- Herbal Garden
- Nature walks
- Arunachala presence
- Yoga / Meditation / Ayurveda as part of the stay experience

Descriptions require client confirmation where factual details are missing.

### 07 Stay & Packages
Journey types from the architecture:
- Short Stay
- Wellness Stay
- Monthly Stay
- Wellness packages: 7 / 14 / 21 days were presented strategically, but final durations/package names must be confirmed before being presented as current offerings.

### 08 Trust
Architecture calls for:
- Founder
- Practitioners
- Guest Stories
- Real Photography

Only verified content.

### 09 Blog / Journal
The blog replaces the static gallery and acts as an educational, SEO and trust-building channel.

### 10 Final enquiry
Convert confidence into a personal conversation.

Primary: WhatsApp
Secondary: email/enquiry form

## User journey

The supplied architecture describes:

`Discover -> Understand -> Connect -> Nourish -> Experience -> Choose -> Trust -> Explore -> Plan`

The implementation should map this to:

`Hero -> Wellness -> Arunachala -> Food -> Resort -> Experience -> Stay -> Trust -> Blog -> Enquire`

## Page responsibilities

### Wellness
Explain the broader wellness philosophy and Ayurveda as one part of it.

### Stay
Explain accommodation and stay patterns, without fake hotel inventory.

### Experience
Show the character of the property beyond accommodation or treatment.

### About
Humanize the brand, story, philosophy and people.

### Blog
Educate, build familiarity, improve international discovery/SEO, and show the experience before arrival.

### Contact
Make direct contact simple and show verified location/contact information.

### Enquire
Provide a premium enquiry experience rather than a booking engine.
